Joint List leader Ahmad Tibi says the Arab-majority party cannot be ignored after historic turnout in Israel's election. Exit polls suggest no clear winner and a rebuke for Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, though he is described as far from defeated. Tibi has outlined a plan to oust Netanyahu if neither bloc forms a majority.
